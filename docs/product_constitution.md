# B1.2 Product Constitution

## Company definition

A Personal Financial Intelligence platform that continuously understands a customer's financial state, helps them improve it, and connects them to suitable financial products at the right time.

## Two acquisition doors

### Door A: Get More From My Money

Quick value: identify value leakage, rewards/card mismatch, fees, subscriptions, avoidable interest and inefficient money usage.

### Door B: Borrow Better

Quick value: estimate comfortable EMI, comfortable borrowing range, debt pressure and credit readiness before the customer borrows.

## Shared diagnostic layer

Financial Health is not a separate acquisition door. It is a shared diagnostic layer surfaced only after the customer receives initial value.

## Fulfilment split

### Build now

- Financial state diagnosis
- Affordability
- Comfortable EMI/range
- Credit readiness
- Borrow less/wait/repay-first recommendations
- Rewards/value leakage diagnostics

### Gate before build/release

- Loan offers
- Lender ranking
- Apply-to-lender handoff
- Any partner/LSP/DLA workflow

## Master principle

1A earns the right to ask for more. 1B earns the right to recommend. Fulfilment earns the revenue.
